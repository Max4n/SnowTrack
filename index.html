    About your data

    Location is read only while tracking is on. Sessions are stored on this phone and never uploaded. The only network call is the weather lookup, which sends the ski area's coordinates, not yours.

    Safety

    Stay on marked pistes, follow the FIS code and don't use your phone while skiing. Speed and descent come from GPS and can be off by several metres.
